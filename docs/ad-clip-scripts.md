# 3DBuildBot Ad Clip Scripts

24 short-form video scripts (15–30 sec each) for social ads, product-page loops, and email embeds. Hand off to a video-production session or editor.

## Ground rules (please respect)

- **Do NOT claim** ISO 9001, AS9100, ITAR/DDTC registration, or specific customer names (Rivian, Anduril, Northrop, etc.). Those claims were pulled from the site because the company doesn't yet hold them.
- **Do NOT invent** stats we can't back up (parts shipped, uptime, on-time rate).
- **True today**: instant FDM quotes, 3–6 day FDM shipping via US supplier network, hand-quotes for SLA/SLS/MJF/CNC in 1 business day, AI DFM feedback, in-browser CAD parsing, locked 30-day pricing, free tools + guides.
- **Voice**: senior engineer talking to a peer. Confident, specific, no hype adjectives ("revolutionary", "game-changing", "cutting-edge" are banned).
- **Word-count target**: 15s ≈ 40 words · 20s ≈ 55 words · 25s ≈ 70 words · 30s ≈ 85 words. Trim, don't stretch.
- **Every clip ends** with a URL card: `3dbuildbot.com` in the corner or held at end.

## Delivery instructions for the video editor

1. **Format**: 9:16 vertical for social (TikTok, Reels, Shorts), 16:9 for LinkedIn/YouTube pre-roll. Cut both versions of every clip.
2. **Captions**: burn in — 60%+ of viewers watch muted.
3. **Logo watermark**: bottom-right, 60% opacity, 24pt.
4. **URL**: hold `3dbuildbot.com` on screen for last 2 seconds of every clip.
5. **Consistent color grade**: cool blues + warm whites, ~5600K, saturation −10.
6. **Music budget**: use Artlist / Epidemic Sound with proper license — no YouTube-audio-library since we'll run these as paid ads.
7. **Test file**: cut one clip (recommend A1 or D1) first and share back before batch-producing all 24 — check pacing, captions, color grade.
